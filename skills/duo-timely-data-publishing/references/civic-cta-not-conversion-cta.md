# Civic CTA, Not Conversion CTA

## Concept

Publishing on a live news event buys attention you did not pay for, and the price of that attention is that every element of the piece gets read as evidence of motive. The body copy is rarely the problem. The call to action is. A reader who accepts your number as interesting will still judge the piece by what you asked them to do at the end of it, and if the answer is "sign up," the whole thing retroactively reads as opportunism. That is the specific failure mode that flips earned coverage from neutral to hostile, and it is avoided by routing the ask to somebody other than yourself.

## What Duolingo does

Source: blog.duolingo.com/ukraine-language (Duolingo blog, 2022-03-02; accessed 2026-09-22)

- Published days after the February 2022 invasion, leading with its own enrollment signal — a **200% increase in people studying Ukrainian between the week of February 14 and the week of February 21, 2022**.
- The body is an explainer, not a promotion — how Ukrainian and Russian relate as languages, written by people with the standing to say so. The proprietary number is the hook; the expertise is the payload.
- Framed the surge in the reader's terms — "perhaps even an act of solidarity" — rather than as demand for a product.
- Closed by pointing readers to **Translators without Borders and UNICEF USA**. No paid tier, no upgrade prompt, no signup link at the bottom.

The economics are the point. A usage spike on a news event is free distribution that no owned-media budget can buy, and the only way to lose it is to price it. Giving up the conversion at the end of one post is a rounding error against the coverage; capturing it is how you end up as the example in somebody else's story about brands exploiting a crisis.

## The transferable pattern

Three rules for publishing into a serious moment:

1. **Lead with the number only you can see, then spend the piece explaining it.** Your telemetry earns you the right to speak. Your explanation is what makes the piece worth reading. Reversing the order makes it an ad with a statistic in it.
2. **Send the reader somewhere that is not you.** A relief organization, a regulator, a public dataset, a helpline, a standards body. The recommendation should be one you would make if you sold nothing.
3. **Match the register to the severity.** A piece about a television show can be playful. A piece about a war cannot, and the same house voice that usually earns you goodwill will cost you here.

The general form: when the moment is somebody else's, make your contribution the information and let the action belong to them. You are trading one conversion for standing, and standing compounds.

## Apply to your product

- If a crisis in your category moved your numbers tomorrow, who would you point readers to instead of your own signup — and do you already have that relationship?
- Which parts of your usual publishing template (the upsell block, the house jokes, the footer CTA) would you strip out, and who decides that under time pressure?
- What is a conversion from one post actually worth to you, against the cost of being cited as the company that tried?
