# Port the Method and the Format, Vary the Subject

## Concept

When you enter an adjacent domain, the temptation is to start over because you lack expertise there. Usually the wrong instinct. What made the original work was rarely subject knowledge — it was a method for taking someone from nothing to competence, and a content format your audience has learned to trust. Both are machinery, and machinery transfers. What must come from the new domain is the vocabulary, because that is the one thing your buyer already has fixed opinions about.

## What Duolingo does

Source: blog.duolingo.com/chess-course (Duolingo blog, 2025-06-10; accessed 2026-09-22)

- Duolingo ported its existing teaching method into chess rather than inventing a chess pedagogy — **75% of the course is puzzle-based**, lessons shift from guided ("move your bishop here") to open-ended ("find checkmate in two moves"), spaced repetition revisits earlier tactics, and content was cut to essentials so a learner **can play a real game by the end of Section 1**.
- The end-state is external and concrete — the course targets **~1500 Elo**, a recognized strong-intermediate rating, rather than a completion percentage that means nothing outside the app. This was the **first new subject launch since Math and Music in 2023**.
- **Tension.** The stated wedge is that existing chess platforms are built for competitive players rather than learners, so the method's advantage is concentrated at the beginner end. Duolingo explicitly gated further investment — Elo placement tests, real-time coaching — on the course succeeding first.
- The content format ported too, unchanged. Chess explainers use the identical shape as the grammar guides — an "In this post" table of contents, a 101 section, an etymology hook (rook from the Persian *rukh*, "chariot"), diagrams, explicit **8 Do's and 4 Don'ts**, a mid-post and end-post call to action, and a pun sign-off. Source: blog.duolingo.com/rook-chess-piece (Duolingo blog, 2026-05-21; accessed 2026-09-22)
- The naming does not port. Duolingo Math's 8th grade course is aligned to national standards and its units carry **the names teachers and parents already look for** — slope-intercept lines, intro to functions, the Pythagorean theorem, bivariate data. Source: blog.duolingo.com/8th-grade-math (Duolingo blog, 2026-07-14; accessed 2026-09-22)

## The transferable pattern

Split your product into three parts before entering a new domain.

1. **The method** — how you take someone from zero to capable. Practice over explanation, review timed against forgetting, scaffolding from guided to open-ended, ruthless cutting to essentials. This encodes how people acquire skill, not what the skill is, so it ports before you have deep domain expertise.
2. **The format** — the shape of your artifacts. A format that has earned trust carries that trust across topics, and reusing it means you are not asking people to learn a new structure at the exact moment you are asking them to accept a new offering. Inventing a second format also splits your own production capacity in half.
3. **The vocabulary** — names of units, levels, categories. This comes from the destination, not from you. Anyone evaluating you arrives with a fixed list of requirements in their own words; if your labels differ, every evaluator has to build the crosswalk themselves, and most will not pay that cost. Matching their taxonomy moves the mapping work onto you, once, and makes coverage self-evident at a glance.

Give the new line an **external end-state** rather than an internal progress bar — a recognized rating, a certification threshold, a concrete capability someone outside your product would acknowledge. And gate deeper investment on the first version working, rather than building the advanced tier in parallel on faith.

## Apply to your product

- Write your method down as steps with no reference to your current subject matter. Does anything survive that isn't just "we have good content"?
- What is your standard artifact shape, and could you produce one in the new domain this week without inventing anything new?
- What taxonomy does your new buyer already use to evaluate options — and are your units named for it, or for your internal architecture?
