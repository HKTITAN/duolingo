# Character System

## Concept

A character system is more than one mascot — it's a roster of characters, each with a distinct visual identity, pose set, animation rig, and voice register, deployed consistently across every product surface. Done right, it converts every screen into an emotional moment and gives the brand a face users recognize before they read the logo.

This is the design implementation of [[../duo-voice/references/character-archetypes]].

## What Duolingo does

Source: blog.duolingo.com/building-character (Duolingo blog, 2020-11-10; accessed 2026-09-22) and blog.duolingo.com/world-character-visemes (Duolingo blog, 2022-11-10; accessed 2026-09-22)

- Ten World Characters, built over 18 months of design work before they shipped.
- They were deliberately placed "in places where learners spent most of their time" — designed into the majority of exercises as the speaker of the sentence being translated, with a unique per-character animation on every correct answer and interstitials rewarding correct streaks. An expensive asset behind a rare trigger is an asset nobody sees.
- Each character carries its own full viseme set — 20+ mouth shapes — designed to stay faithful to the house aesthetic while still reading as that character. Lip-sync timing is generated by running synthesized speech through in-house speech-recognition and pronunciation models to get per-phoneme timing, across 40+ languages and 100+ courses.
- The rig is a Rive State Machine, chosen to turn a limited number of assets into a virtually unlimited number of combinations at a file size that runs on Android, iOS, and Web.
- Concept scope is part of the system: vikings and space aliens were explored and rejected as too far removed from the reality of language learning. The cast is modern humans with very quirky personalities.

**The cost is real, and corrections are forward-only.** When Vikram's spherical torso plus frequent food illustrations read as a harmful Sikh stereotype, the fix required a Sikh sensitivity consultant, a volunteer committee of South Asian employees, positive reference gathering, and a reshape from circles to a pill form — and the post concedes "we won't be able to update every past instance of Vikram in the app" (blog.duolingo.com/vikram-redesign (Duolingo blog, 2024-04-04; accessed 2026-09-22)). Every character you ship is a liability you may later only be able to correct going forward.

## The transferable pattern

Five components of a character system:

1. **At least two characters in productive contrast** — one alone is a mascot; two or more is a system.
2. **A pose set per character** — covering the emotions the product needs to convey.
3. **A rig** — characters that can animate, not just pose, integrate into motion.
4. **Surface placement rules** — documented mapping of which character appears where.
5. **Voice paired with face** — see [[../duo-voice/references/character-archetypes]].

The cost is real: illustration, rigging, animation, copy. Don't half-build a character system. A weakly-built mascot is worse than no mascot — it occupies the slot and produces nothing.

## Apply to your product

- Do you have a character? If yes, does it have a pose set, a rig, or just a logo?
- Could a second character add productive contrast (different register, different role)?
- Where in your product would a character improve the experience? Where would it feel forced?
