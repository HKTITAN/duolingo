# Duolingo Expert Content Engine — Map of Content

Most companies have real expertise and no way to publish it repeatably. They produce campaigns, not programs: a burst of posts, an empty topic list, silence. Duolingo solved the supply problem structurally — the questions users already send in become the topic queue, a named credentialed expert answers them on a fixed cadence, and the submission address at the bottom of every instalment refills the queue.

The second half is less obvious and buys more trust than the answers do: marking in public where the evidence stops. Defining the unit before publishing a count. Naming the motivated-reasoning risk before answering a contested question. Saying plainly that nobody knows.

## Boundaries

This skill owns expertise-driven, question-fed publishing. It does **not** own content whose payload is your own usage data — the annual report, the state-by-state map, the spike post — and it does not own whether a number was validly measured in the first place. Pixel craft, motion, and design tokens route out of this pack entirely.
