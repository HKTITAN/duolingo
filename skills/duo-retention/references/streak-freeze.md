# Streak Freeze

## Concept

A streak freeze is a token that absorbs a missed day, preserving the streak counter. It looks like generosity. It is, in fact, the most calculated retention move in the whole system.

The mechanic exists because the streak is so harsh that without it, a single bad week causes total churn — the user breaks the streak, loses the [[loss-aversion]] anchor, and has nothing pulling them back.

## What Duolingo does

- Users can hold a small number of streak freezes at a time — typically two — earned through engagement or purchasable.
- A freeze auto-applies on a missed day; the user wakes up and sees the counter intact.
- The supply is intentionally scarce; freezes feel valuable, not unlimited, which keeps the underlying action incentive intact.

## The transferable pattern

Three reasons to add a freeze-equivalent to any streak product:

1. **It preserves loss aversion.** A user with a 200-day streak still has a 200-day streak; the asymmetric pain of losing it stays intact.
2. **It removes guilt-driven churn.** Without a freeze, a single miss often leads to: *"I broke it, why bother."* With a freeze, that branch doesn't exist.
3. **It lowers the bar to start a streak in the first place.** New users are less afraid to try if they know the system has give.

The freeze is strictly a retention move dressed as a kindness. Don't price it as charity; price it as scarcity.

## Apply to your product

- If you have a streak (or a counter, or a habit chain), what happens on a miss? Total reset is rarely the right answer.
- How could a small, scarce safety valve absorb the bad day without diluting the daily incentive?
- What's the cost of *not* having one — how many users churn after one miss?
