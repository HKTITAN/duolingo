# Displace an Accredited Incumbent

## Concept

When an incumbent's position rests on accreditation rather than on product quality, you cannot win by being better — buyers are not allowed to switch to an unvalidated instrument for a consequential decision. But they can afford to use one where the cost of being wrong is only a second look. Entering upstream, as a cheap first-stage filter, gets you adopted before you are accredited. It also has the buyer run both instruments on the same population, which produces exactly the paired dataset that proves equivalence — at the incumbent's expense rather than yours. Then the accreditation has to already be in hand when the incumbent's infrastructure breaks, because approval processes cannot be compressed under pressure.

## What Duolingo does

- **Got used before it was accepted.** Carnegie Mellon's Kigali campus ran the Duolingo English Test at the *start* of admissions while still officially requiring TOEFL or IELTS, so only the most likely candidates had to pay for the expensive test. That workflow produced a paired dataset showing DET scores correlated with IELTS at **r = 0.834**, with **97% of applicants** submitting IELTS scores and **90,000+ DET test takers** in the first year. The access problem was stark: IELTS cost **$200** and TOEFL **$250** in Kigali against a 2014 Rwandan per-capita income of **$741** — roughly four months' income. Source: blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-english-test-africa-ielts-and-cefr (Duolingo blog, 2015-08-14; accessed 2026-09-22)
- **Was already accredited when the incumbent's infrastructure failed.** When test centers closed en masse in Vietnam in November 2022 and during China's lockdowns, the DET was already accepted by **4,000+ academic programs in 100+ countries**, including all of the top 25 highest-enrolling U.S. universities for international students. **China test takers nearly doubled year over year**; test takers came from **213+ countries and 20,000+ cities**; India was the largest single source; **80+ Irish programs** accepted it for study visas. Source: blog.duolingo.com/det-year-in-review-2022 (Duolingo blog, 2022-12-14; accessed 2026-09-22)

Tension: the correlation was surfaced by the customer, not by a designed validity study, and the sample is self-selected — one institution's applicant pool. Evidence generated by a buyer's convenience is cheap and persuasive, and it is not the same thing as a validity study. Treat it as the argument that buys you the chance to run a real one.

## The transferable pattern

1. **Enter where being wrong is cheap.** Find the step upstream of the consequential decision, where your output only triggers a second look. Adoption there does not require accreditation.
2. **Let the buyer generate your evidence.** Running your instrument alongside the incumbent on one population is the paired dataset you could never afford to commission.
3. **Price against the access cost, not the incumbent's list price.** If the incumbent costs a meaningful fraction of a buyer's annual income, the market you are entering is the people currently excluded, and it is larger than the served one.
4. **Do the slow acceptance work before the shock.** Adoption during a supply disruption is decided by what a buyer can accept *that week*. Approvals take months and do not compress.

## Apply to your product

- Where in your buyer's process is there a step whose cost of error is only rework? Could you be adopted there without approval?
- If a buyer ran you and the incumbent side by side for a year, what dataset would that produce, and would it prove your case?
- What approval, certification or listing would you need in hand before a disruption in your category — and have you started it?
