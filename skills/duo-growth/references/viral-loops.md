# Viral Loops

## Concept

A viral loop is a mechanic where the act of using the product naturally produces invitations or exposure to non-users. The lever isn't "ask users to share" — it's *design the mechanic so that not sharing is harder than sharing*. Done well, growth becomes a function of retention; done badly, it produces spam and brand damage.

## What Duolingo does

- **Friend leaderboards** — competitive streaks and league standings work better when you can see friends. Adding friends is a casual, organic ask.
- **Streak share screens** — milestones produce shareable moments at week boundaries (7/30/100/365 days), each timed to land when the user is at peak emotional payoff.
- **Story-format wraps** — yearly recaps users post to social, designed to fit standard story formats with character cameos.
- **The product surface itself is shareable** — screenshots of weird notifications, character moments, and lessons spread because the brand is the loop.

## The transferable pattern

Three rules:

1. **Tie the loop to a real user moment.** Sharing a streak milestone is honest because the user did the thing. Sharing for sharing's sake is spam.
2. **Design the artifact, not just the trigger.** A streak share screen with no thought to what the *received* version looks like is half-built.
3. **Make non-sharing a default, not a friction.** The user must affirmatively share. Sneaking shares into actions is brand damage.

Anti-pattern: viral loops that demand an invitation to access core value (LinkedIn early-2010s style). Short-term growth, long-term brand damage.

## Apply to your product

- Does any real user moment in your product produce a natural share opportunity?
- If you have viral loops, are they share-by-default or affirmative-share?
- Could a moment your users already feel proud of be turned into a shareable artifact?
