# Fixed window, repeated forever

## Concept

A one-off data drop is a news cycle. The same measurement, taken over the same calendar window, published on roughly the same date every year, becomes a time series — and a time series lets a journalist write "X changed" instead of "X is". The compounding is the point: every new edition retroactively increases the value of all prior editions, because each one is now a baseline. A competitor with more money can buy a survey; they cannot buy six years of identically-windowed history. The discipline that produces this is boring and non-negotiable: do not move the window, do not redefine the unit, do not skip a year.

## What Duolingo does

- The Duolingo Language Report has run annually since 2020, reaching **6 consecutive editions (2020 through 2025)**. The 2025 edition states its window as **1 October 2024 to 30 September 2025** — the same 1 October to 30 September window used in prior editions — aggregates by country or language for privacy, and republishes the whole report in local languages including Polish, Turkish, Indonesian, Thai, Chinese and Hindi.
  Source: blog.duolingo.com/2025-duolingo-language-report (Duolingo blog, 2025-12-01; accessed 2026-09-22)
- Each edition links back to prior years and frames its findings as movement against them, which is only possible because the window did not move: Japanese overtaking Italian, Korean displaced by Italian's return, Chinese climbing from 9th to 8th. The 2024 edition restates the same **1 October to 30 September** window and the same **5,000-learner minimum per country**.
  Source: blog.duolingo.com/2024-duolingo-language-report (Duolingo blog, 2024-12-02; accessed 2026-09-22)
- The recurring US map post shows the same discipline at a smaller scale, published in **2017, 2020 and 2021** in a reader-question column format, with the year-over-year change carried as the actual narrative rather than a footnote. The repetition is what made the second story free: the delta needed no new data collection, only the prior edition.
  Source: blog.duolingo.com/whats-the-most-popular-language-in-the-united-states (Duolingo blog, 2022-02-08; accessed 2026-09-22)
- Tension: a frozen window is a commitment you will eventually resent. If the metric you chose in edition one turns out to be the wrong metric, improving it breaks the comparability that is the whole asset — and a company-shaping event that lands outside your window (a launch, an acquisition, a crisis) is invisible until the next edition. The only clean escape is to add a new series alongside the old one and run both until the new one has its own history.

## The transferable pattern

Treat the report as infrastructure with a version contract, not as a campaign.

1. **Freeze four things in edition one:** the start and end dates of the window, the aggregation unit, the exclusion floor, and the metric definitions. Write them down as a spec, not as prose in a post.
2. **Publish on a predictable date.** The recurrence is what turns coverage into a calendar entry that gets written up without a pitch.
3. **Link every edition to the previous one explicitly.** That link is what tells a reader a series exists and is what makes the archive discoverable.
4. **Never skip an edition.** A gap does not cost you one data point; it costs you every delta that crosses the gap, permanently.
5. **When you must change a definition, add rather than replace.** Run the old series and the new one in parallel for at least two editions, and publish both, so nobody has to guess which break was methodological.
6. **Budget for the unglamorous years.** Some editions will have nothing surprising in them. Publish anyway — the value of edition four is mostly realised in edition six.

## Apply to your product

- What twelve-month window could you commit to measuring identically for the next five years, and who owns the spec when the person who wrote it leaves?
- If you publish once and stop, what exactly have you built? If you publish six times, what does the sixth edition let you say that the first could not?
- Which metric definition are you least confident about, and would you rather fix it now or be locked into it for five editions?
