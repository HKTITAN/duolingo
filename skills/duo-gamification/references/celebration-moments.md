# Celebration Moments

## Concept

A celebration is the high-amplitude version of [[juicy-feedback]] — reserved for boundary events (session complete, level up, streak milestone, league promotion). It's louder, longer, and frequently character-driven. The discipline: rare enough that it lands, frequent enough that the user looks forward to the next one.

## What Duolingo does

- **Lesson end:** XP tally with motion, character reaction, sound, milestone-style summary.
- **Streak milestones:** distinct visual treatment at 7, 30, 50, 100, 365 days; each different.
- **League promotion:** layered animation with characters, leaderboard reshuffle, occasional treasure chest.
- **Level up:** unit completion gets a guidebook reveal or unit-end gauntlet.

The frequency is calibrated: small celebrations after every lesson, bigger ones at week boundaries, peak ones at long-term milestones.

## The transferable pattern

Three rules:

1. **Three tiers, not one.** Tiny per-action feedback ([[juicy-feedback]]), medium per-session celebration, large per-milestone celebration. Without tiers, every event feels the same and nothing lands.
2. **Distinct grammar per tier.** Tiny = motion + sound. Medium = animation sequence + character. Large = full-screen takeover. Don't blur the lines.
3. **Anticipation matters.** The user should be able to feel "the big one is coming" — the streak counter showing 6 with the 7-day milestone visible is itself part of the celebration.

Anti-pattern: a single full-screen celebration after every action. Users learn to skip it.

## Apply to your product

- Do you have three tiers of celebration, or one (or none)?
- What's your "big" celebration moment? Could a user describe it from memory?
- Where in your product is anticipation visible? Where is it missing?
